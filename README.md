## Hi there, I'm Paul Bohm 👋

I'm building a new programming language and runtime for the machine age.
My key interest is in how the design of systems changes what people and machines can do together.

## Background

I'm an engineer and founder with a background in distributed systems and computer security.

- Dropbox — Designed and implemented the LAN Sync peer-to-peer protocol as an early engineer.
- Teleport — Founded a decentralized ridesharing company built around an open network.
- mjam — Co-founded the Austrian food delivery company, later acquired by Delivery Hero.
- [Deepsec](https://deepsec.net/) - Started and sold a computer-security conference.
- Metalab & hackerspaces.org — Founded Vienna's Metalab and the hackerspaces.org community.
- Quantum cryptography — Co-authored [*Secure Quantum Key Growing without Privacy Amplification* (2004) with Hannes R. Böhm, Markus Aspelmeyer, Časlav Brukner, and Anton Zeilinger.](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0408179)
